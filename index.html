What happens next?

ROADGUARD's proposed workflow.

01

Report

Share category, location and optional details.

02

Verify

Check freshness and corroborating reports.

03

Alert

Show proximity warnings without distracting drivers.

04

Resolve

Authorized teams acknowledge and close reports.

Trust rule: Clearly label unverified reports. Expired or resolved hazards should not remain active warnings.
Practical student MVP

From local report to better road awareness

Start with a small, testable workflow before adding predictive models or large-scale integrations.

01

Report safely

Choose a hazard and landmark. Add coordinates when available.

02

Corroborate

Use time, location and repeat reports to support review, not guarantee safety.

03

Warn nearby users

A backend can calculate proximity and deliver audio-first alerts.

04

Track resolution

An authority dashboard can prioritize recurring issues and record action.

Deployment note: This is a front-end demo. A production system needs a backend API, persistent database, authentication, moderation, notifications, and agreements with relevant authorities. Do not present demo entries as verified real-world hazards.
